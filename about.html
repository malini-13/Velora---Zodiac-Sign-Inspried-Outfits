About Velora

ABOUT VELORA

Velora treats astrology as a fashion language: poetic, symbolic and always anchored in the garment.

Our story

We began with a question.

What if your sign did not just describe you, but dressed you? Velora was built to answer that with one collection per zodiac: twelve identities, twelve silhouettes, one unmistakable point of view.

The philosophy

Your zodiac is more than a birth date.

It is an identity, an energy and a language of self-expression. Velora translates that language into fashion that feels luxury-forward, emotionally sharp and visually composed.

The twelve signs

One piece per sign.

Each zodiac receives a signature look to keep the idea clear and editorial. The result is a focused brand system rather than a crowded catalog of loosely themed products.

Our aesthetic

Mythology, celestial restraint and modern dress.

We blend gothic elegance, subtle celestial symbolism, dark fantasy atmosphere and contemporary tailoring. The zodiac appears as a detail, not a costume.

Final statement

Velora does not decorate the zodiac.
It dresses it.

A fashion label with mythology in its bones and restraint in its silhouette.
